# SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (SRS v4.0)
**Project Title:** StaySuite AI — Offline-First Hospitality & Resort Operating System
**Selected Architecture (Path A):** Flutter (Android/iOS/Windows/macOS) + Next.js (Guest & QR Web) + FastAPI (Modular Monolith) + Drift Local SQLite + PostgreSQL
**Target Market:** Coastal Homestays (Konkan Belt), Beach Resorts, Boutique Hotels, Restaurants & Bars

## 1. Executive Summary & System Overview
StaySuite AI is an offline-first, multi-category SaaS platform engineered for Indian homestays and resorts. It eliminates reception bottlenecks, unpaid OTA bookings, kitchen food waste, and checkout billing chaos using a **"One Brain, Three Faces"** architecture:
1. **The Core Brain (FastAPI + PostgreSQL):** A modular monolith backend supporting multi-category plugins (Homestay, Resort, Hotel, Restaurant, Bar) without code conflicts.
2. **Face 1 — Owner & Manager POS/PMS App (Flutter Native Mobile + Desktop):** A single Dart codebase compiled natively to Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS with an embedded local SQLite database (`Drift`) for <5ms response times, 100% offline billing, and sub-second Bluetooth/USB ESC/POS thermal printing.
3. **Face 2 — Guest Zero-Install Web + WhatsApp Bot (Next.js SSR + Meta Cloud API):** Fast mobile web links for direct bookings, 30-second walk-in QR self-check-in, 50% UPI advance payments, and cut-off-enforced meal pre-ordering.
4. **Face 3 — Staff & Kitchen Frictionless Interface (Door QR + WhatsApp + Marathi/Hindi Audio):** Behind-the-door laminated QR stickers for 1-second room cleaning updates and automated morning market shopping lists.

## 2. Module-by-Module Functional Requirements

### Module 1: Business-Category Plugin Engine, Dynamic Rooms & 360° Media
* **FR-1.1 Category Plugin System:** Onboarding configures `properties.business_config` (JSONB), activating Homestay rules (24-hr/4-hr meal cut-offs, AC/Non-AC toggle) while hiding unrelated modules (e.g., Bar liquor inventory) until enabled.
* **FR-1.2 AC vs. Non-AC Dynamic Tariff Toggle:** Every room stores `base_tariff_ac` and `base_tariff_non_ac`. Toggling "AC Required" off on the booking/check-in screen recalculates the folio immediately and dispatches a staff alert to keep the AC off.
* **FR-1.3 Extra Person & Driver Dormitory Fee Waive:** Supports fixed charges for extra mattresses and driver beds with a 1-click Manager/Owner `[Waive in Bill]` toggle during checkout.
* **FR-1.4 Plan-Gated Visual Media:**
  * *Standard Tier:* Fast WebP photo gallery + room floor/bed metadata.
  * *Premium Tier:* Interactive 360° equirectangular panorama viewer, Instagram Creator Reel embeds, and Google Street View integration.
* **FR-1.5 Global AI Settings Search & Action Bar:** A command bar at the top of the Flutter app accepts voice/text in Marathi, Hindi, or English (e.g., *"Freeze rooms for Dec 24"*, *"Change Sea-Facing price to ₹4,000"*) and surfaces a 1-click confirmation action card.

### Module 2: Direct Storefront, Walk-In QR & Booking.com "Unpaid" Bridge
* **FR-2.1 Zero-Commission Storefront:** Auto-generates `staysuite.in/{property-slug}` for Google Maps, Instagram bio, and WhatsApp auto-replies.
* **FR-2.2 Dual Walk-In Desk Mode:** Managers can log a walk-in via a 3-field quick form on the Flutter app OR have the guest scan a Reception QR code to submit details and ID on their phone in 30 seconds.
* **FR-2.3 1-Click Emergency Room Freeze:** A master toggle in the Flutter app immediately blocks selected rooms on the direct storefront and pushes zero-availability updates to connected OTAs.
* **FR-2.4 Automated OTA Unpaid Booking Workflow:** When an unpaid Booking.com reservation arrives (via Email Parser / iCal in Phase 1, or Channex.io two-way API in Phase 2), the system automatically sends a WhatsApp message to the guest with:
  1. A 50% UPI Advance Payment Link, and
  2. A mandatory Homestay Food Pre-Order Rule acknowledgment checkbox.

### Module 3: Category-Aware Dining, Unified Catalog & Market Calculator
* **FR-3.1 Automated Meal Cut-Off Gatekeeper:** Enforces 24-hour pre-arrival and 4-hour in-stay ordering windows for fresh seafood/thalis. Late orders display: *"Fresh kitchen prep is closed for this slot — Request Manager Override OR View Instant Snacks."*
* **FR-3.2 Single Unified Catalog:** Adding or updating a dish in the Flutter app updates both the Next.js Guest Menu and the POS Billing catalog simultaneously.
* **FR-3.3 Deterministic Recipe BOM Market Calculator:** Uses exact PostgreSQL arithmetic (`Confirmed Orders × Recipe BOM Weight - Current Kitchen Stock`) to generate a morning Market Shopping List for the buyer (Abhijeet) grouped by Fish Market and Veg Market, and Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) for the cook (Vedika).

### Module 4 & 5: Vyapar++ Split Billing, Thermal KOT, Khata & Auto-Kirana
* **FR-4.1 Partial Room Checkout (Group Split Billing):** Decouples `bookings` from `booking_room_allocations` and `folios`. If a group books 3 rooms and 1 room checks out a day early, the Manager performs a Partial Checkout for that single room while keeping the other 2 rooms active.
* **FR-4.2 Sub-Second Bluetooth & USB ESC/POS Thermal Printing:** Native byte-stream printing on 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) thermal printers for Kitchen KOTs and Final Checkout Bills with an embedded Dynamic UPI QR Code.
* **FR-4.3 Customer Khata (Credit Ledger):** Moves unpaid balances of trusted guests to a credit ledger that sends automated WhatsApp UPI payment reminders until settled.
* **FR-5.1 10-Category Expense Tracker & Auto-Kirana List:** Tracks Fish/Meat, Vegetables, Kirana, Room Amenities, Cleaning Supplies, Petrol, Electricity, Water, Wi-Fi, and LPG. Automatically compiles a Kirana Purchase List whenever stock falls below `min_threshold_qty`.

### Module 6: Legal-Grade Police Compliance & Role-Based Privacy Masking
* **FR-6.1 Strict Role-Based ID Redaction:**
  * *Manager & Staff Login:* Sensitive government IDs are strictly hidden and displayed only as `[Aadhaar Redacted]` alongside verification status so temporary staff cannot misuse guest data.
  * *Owner & Police Inspection Portal:* Protected by 2FA and audit logs, with 1-click official Police Register PDF/Excel export and Foreign Guest Form-C alerts.

### Module 7: Frictionless Staff Door QR, WhatsApp Tasks & Payroll/Upaad
* **FR-7.1 Behind-the-Door QR Housekeeping:** Scanning the laminated QR sticker inside a room door opens a mobile web page with Marathi/Hindi voice support and a giant `[ 🟢 Room Cleaned ]` button that flips the owner's dashboard from Red to Green in 1 second.
* **FR-7.2 Multi-Model Payroll & Upaad (Advance) Ledger:** Supports Monthly Fixed, Daily Wage, Off-Season Part-Time, Per-Room Bonus, and Peak-Season Intern Stipends, automatically deducting mid-month cash advances (*Upaad*) from final payouts.

### Module 8: Startup SaaS Pricing, 30-Day Trial & Zero-MDR Payments
* **FR-8.1 Trial, Custom Deals & Referral Engine:** 30-day full-feature trial, Super Admin custom Setup Fee + Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) overrides, and automated Owner-to-Owner Referral discounts.
* **FR-8.2 Dual Payment Architecture:** Direct Dynamic UPI QR (`upi://pay?...`) for 0% MDR guest-to-owner bill payments, plus Razorpay/Cashfree Subscriptions for automated SaaS recurring billing and instant webhook-verified OTA advances.

PART 3: Offline Sync & Thermal Printer Connection Architecture
3.1 How Local SQLite (Drift) Syncs with Cloud PostgreSQL (FastAPI)
To ensure the Flutter app works smoothly during coastal internet blackouts and syncs without data loss:

Every Table Has 3 Sync Columns: updated_at (TIMESTAMPTZ), is_deleted (BOOLEAN), and sync_status ('SYNCED' | 'PENDING_PUSH').

Local Write + Outbox Queue: When the Manager creates a walk-in booking, adds food to a folio, or performs a Partial Checkout, Flutter writes directly to the local Drift SQLite database (<5ms) and inserts a JSON mutation event into the local sync_outbox table.

Push Engine (POST /api/v1/sync/push): Whenever connectivity_plus detects internet access, the background sync worker sends batched sync_outbox mutations to FastAPI in chronological order. FastAPI applies them inside an atomic PostgreSQL transaction and returns 200 OK.

Delta Pull Engine (GET /api/v1/sync/pull?since=last_pulled_timestamp): Every 30 seconds (or immediately when triggered by a FastAPI WebSocket event like a guest scanning a Door QR), Flutter fetches only records where updated_at > last_pulled_timestamp and upserts them into local SQLite.

3.2 Unified Thermal Printer Pipeline (Mobile Bluetooth + Desktop USB)
Step 1 (Byte Generation): Using esc_pos_utils_plus, a single ReceiptGenerator class in Dart converts the KOT or Split Checkout Bill (including the native GS ( k UPI QR command) into a List<int> raw ESC/POS byte array.

Step 2 (Platform Hardware Router):

if (Platform.isAndroid || Platform.isIOS) ➔ Sends List<int> bytes over Bluetooth Classic/BLE via print_bluetooth_thermal.

if (Platform.isWindows || Platform.isMacOS) ➔ Sends List<int> bytes directly to the USB/COM/Spooler port via flutter_pos_printer_platform_image_3.
