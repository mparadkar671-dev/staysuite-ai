StaySuite AI — Master Software Requirements Specification (SRS v4.1)
Architecture: Flutter (StaySuite App Mobile & Desktop) + Next.js 15 (Booking Site & Portals) + FastAPI (Modular Monolith Core Brain) + Drift Local SQLite + PostgreSQL 16

1. Complete 3-Pillar Ecosystem Overview
Pillar 1: Main Business Model (Super Admin / SaaS Control Plane)
* Property Onboarding & Provisioning: Approves new registrations across 6 categories (Homestay, Resort, Hotel, Restaurant, Bar, Other Services) and assigns unique Business Codes (e.g. SS-HM-1001).
* Subscription Plan & Feature Entitlement Matrix: Manages plans (Trial, Standard, Premium, Custom Enterprise) and controls feature flags per tenant.
* Platform Analytics & Global Kill-Switch: Live P&L analytics, subscription fee logs, and 1-click global kill-switch (PATCH /api/v1/superadmin/properties/{id}/status) to suspend/freeze properties or modules.

Pillar 2: Booking Site (Public Marketplace, Owner Onboarding & Customer Portal)
* Multi-Category Public Marketplace (/ and /explore/[category]): Live property showcase with real-time room availability, AC/Non-AC pricing, 24h/4h meal cut-off policies, and amenities.
* Self-Service Owner Listing Wizard (/list-your-property): 3-step wizard with category selection, KYC, subscription payment, and instant issuance of Business Code (SS-XXXX) + Owner credentials.
* Customer Booking & Profile Portal (/property/[slug] and /customer/dashboard): Mobile/Email OTP login, 50% UPI advance checkout, instant receipts, live confirmation tracking, and post-stay reviews.

Pillar 3: StaySuite App (Multi-Tenant, Role-Based Operating System)
* Zero-Rewrite Conditional Category Programming: Single unified codebase where modules (Rooms AC, Pre-Order BOM, Split POS, Housekeeping QR, Staff Upaad, Revenue P&L) are dynamically enabled via runtime feature flags (has_feature) based on category defaults and active subscription plan overrides.
* Multi-Person Role Login: 4-step login (Business Code -> Category Role -> Person Identity -> Password/OTP) supporting multiple workers under the same role (e.g., 3 Cooks, 4 Cleaners, 2 Waiters).
* Role-Specific Dashboards & Universal Self-Service: Dedicated dashboards for Owner, Manager, Housekeeping, Waiter, Cook, Helper, and Accountant, plus universal self-service tabs for My Tasks, My Salary/Upaad, and Leave Requests.

3. 4-Step Multi-Person Role Login Specification
* Step 1: Input Business ID (e.g. SS-HM-1001) -> Validates tenant and retrieves category configuration.
* Step 2: Select Role -> Dynamically returns active roles for that category (Owner, Manager, Staff, Waiter, Cook, Helper, Accountant).
* Step 3: Select Person -> Lists verified team members registered under that role (e.g., Cook -> [Vedika, Suresh]).
* Step 4: Password / OTP -> Authenticates the exact worker and issues a scoped JWT containing tenant_id, role, staff_member_id, and enabled_features[].
* Dual Onboarding: Flow A (Owner creates directly in StaySuite App, ACTIVE) and Flow B (Staff self-registers via app, PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL until approved by Manager/Owner).
